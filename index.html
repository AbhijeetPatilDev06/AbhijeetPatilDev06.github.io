01 / About

Game architect who thinks in systems and streets

I'm Abhijeet Patil, a Technical Game Architect (TGA) and Unity Developer from India. I started making games in 2022 and have spent most of that time on simulation and driving games for Android: tractors, cars, traffic and the worlds they move through.

As founder of Adventurous Mind Games, I built and shipped Indian Tractor Off-Roading, which has passed 90,000 downloads on Google Play. Alongside that I've taken on freelance Unity work, including a real-time multiplayer WebGL game and bug fixing for released titles.

Today I work at Its A Game Studio as Technical Game Architect and Unity Developer on Laila Majnu, where I shape the game's technical structure and plan the districts, road networks and gameplay spaces of a large open-world city.

  • Based in India
  • Engine Unity (C#)
  • Platforms Android, WebGL
  • Focus Open worlds, vehicles, gameplay systems
